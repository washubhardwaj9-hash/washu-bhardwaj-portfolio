About this game

Washu Tic Tac Toe Pro is a feature-rich tic-tac-toe game that goes well beyond the classic 3x3 grid. Players can challenge an AI opponent, play offline, or connect with friends online over the internet — no physical connection or same-device play needed. It includes an in-game friend list, a mailbox system, in-app chat during matches, a store, and animated emojis for reacting mid-game.

Built with Firebase and HTML/JSX, developed entirely on an Android phone using Termux — no PC involved — and packaged into an APK from Termux.

Features

  • MultiplayerPlay with friends online, with in-match chat
  • AI OpponentChallenge a built-in AI, or play a fully offline version
  • SocialFriend list, mailbox, store, and animated emoji reactions
