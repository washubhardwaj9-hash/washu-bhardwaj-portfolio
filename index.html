Washu Tic Tac Toe Pro

Washu Tic Tac Toe Pro is a modern, multiplayer take on classic tic-tac-toe — developed by Washu Bhardwaj, a young game developer and software developer.

Play now GitHub repo
Screenshots coming soon — play the live version above to see it in action

About this game

Washu Tic Tac Toe Pro takes the classic three-in-a-row game and rebuilds it as a full multiplayer experience. Two players can challenge each other online with live in-match chat, or play instantly offline against a built-in AI opponent — no account or internet required for the offline mode.

Beyond the match itself, the game includes a friend list for adding regular opponents, an in-app mailbox, a store, and animated emojis for reacting mid-game. It was designed, built, and tested entirely by Washu Bhardwaj, developed under the WASFORGE Studios identity.

The Studio

WASFORGE Studios

WASFORGE Studios is the independent game-development studio Washu Bhardwaj founded to give his projects a shared identity — every WASFORGE game is designed, built, and tested under one roof. Forging Games. Creating Worlds.

Studio origin

WASFORGE Studios is built around a simple philosophy: start with an idea, turn it into something playable, and keep improving it until it becomes something worth sharing. It's focused on creating original games and interactive experiences while experimenting with ideas across design, programming, and technology.
